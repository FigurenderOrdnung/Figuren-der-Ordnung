DE EN

Figures of Order is published at irregular intervals and brings together essays, portraits, systemic analyses, literary experiments, and archival material. The editors deliberately operate anonymously or under pseudonyms—a phantom text that lays bare thought processes and consciously avoids authorial positions. The names under which the texts appear therefore do not primarily denote biographical identities, but rather voices, roles, and perspectives within a shared editorial space that follows the idea of the drama. *Figures of Order* understands authorship as a form of arrangement: as an attempt to relate thoughts, readings, roles and methods to one another in such a way that a specific form of readability of the present can emerge.

Issue IV: Suspension

Issue IV asks how, under conditions of permanent acceleration, media-driven agitation, and technological logics of execution, spaces can be reclaimed in which perception, judgement, and responsible action become possible.

Suspension does not mean postponement or inactivity. It designates the temporal form of the in-between: the interval in which an event can become legible, a situation open to examination, and a decision capable of being responsibly made.

The issue moves from metaxy, interruption, and institutional forms of suspension through the occupation of the in-between to Kafka, Alexandra Christou, and the political force of weakness.
