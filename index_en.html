Figuren der Ordnung appears at irregular intervals. The issues explore figures of political, aesthetic, and conceptual order.

Issue IV: Deferral

Issue IV examines deferral not as mere delay or weakness, but as a political, philosophical, and aesthetic form within a present shaped by acceleration, enmification, apparatus-driven surprise, and the loss of mediating spaces.

At its centre stands the question of how in-between spaces — of thinking, judgment, governing, and form — can be rethought and defended under the conditions of a darkening world.

The issue unfolds a field of the in-between: metaxu, deferral, interruption, interregnum, figures of weakness, relieved rule, de-singularization, and the residual character of the state.
