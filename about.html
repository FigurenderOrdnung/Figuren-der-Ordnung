About

Figures of Order is an independent journal project bringing together essays, portraits, systemic analyses, literary experiments, and archival material. Published at irregular intervals, it understands itself less as a platform for established positions than as an open space for thought and writing.

At its centre are questions concerning the symbolic, institutional, aesthetic, and political forms through which order emerges, becomes visible, disintegrates, or passes into new media and apparatuses. The issues bring together different modes of writing: theoretical diagnosis, essayistic condensation, literary experiment, and documentary investigation.

Some contributions appear under recurring editorial names. These do not designate authorial profiles in a biographical sense, but gather particular thematic, stylistic, and conceptual perspectives within the project. They mark positions from which writing takes place.

The editors deliberately work anonymously or under pseudonyms. What is meant to become legible is not the figure of the author, but the movement of thought. In this sense, Figures of Order is also an attempt to release texts from the obligation of personal self-disclosure and to allow them to speak as intellectual forms in their own right.

Correspondence, invitations, and enquiries relating to the project are welcome and may be addressed to the editors.

Editorial Voices

Hans Holl writes at the intersection of political theory, cultural diagnosis, and essayistic form. His work centres on figures of the state, institutional symbolism, and the legibility of order.

Jürgen Miller works on questions of political temporality, indirect forms of efficacy, and a theory of waiting and expectation.

Andrea Gaston writes on theatre, scene, gesture, and the relations between body, appearance, and time.

Elena Voss analyses platform regimes, standards, protocols, and forms of digital governmentality.

Arthur writes in a deliberately pointed form on authoritarianism, aestheticised power, and the digital Gesamtkunstwerk.
